Final Draft 13

The industry standard. Not the most convenient and not the cheapest - but its files are what producers expect.

9.2/10
$199.99 - one-time purchase ($249.99 - regular price on the official site)Windows / macOS / iPad / iPhoneFDX - native format30-day trial

Final Draft automatically formats and paginates your script to the Hollywood standard as you type. The app's strength is not the editor itself, but the production built around it: Beat Board for structure, Outline Editor, revision tracking with colored pages, production reports, and page locking.

The weak spots are well known too: the interface looks older than its competitors', collaboration lags well behind cloud editors, and major upgrades are paid - $99-129 - so over 5-7 years a "one-time purchase" costs more than an Arc Studio subscription.

Pros

  • Accepted everywhere: studios, agents, contests
  • Best revision and production-report tools
  • Beat Board and structure inside a single file
  • Stable pagination - one page equals one minute of screen time

Cons

  • Highest entry price
  • Paid major upgrades
  • Collaboration weaker than cloud competitors
  • No Linux or Android version
Verdict: buy it when your scripts genuinely go into production or a studio explicitly asks for it. For a first draft, its power is overkill.
